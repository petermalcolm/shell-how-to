JQ syntax:

For JSON like this:
        {
            "crid": "2733265",
            "nid": {
                "uri": "https://example.org/node/42056",
                "id": "42056",
                "resource": "node",
                "uuid": "c355efa8-bde7-4b89-93b3-fad8b16de304"
            },
            "uid": {
                "uri": "https://example.org/user/35877",
                "id": "35877",
                "resource": "user",
                "uuid": "32c99800-593c-4e22-b29f-d85562115818"
            },
            "date_completed": "1739562088",
            . . . 

use JQ like this:
jq '.list[] | "\(.crid) N: \(.nid.id) U: \(.uid.id)"'

that will result in this:
"2733265 N: 42056 U: 35877"
"2772767 N: 41113 U: 1030"
"2772697 N: 39897 U: 11860"
"2767089 N: 41889 U: 3776"
. . .
